The day, step by step

A one-way traverse: Naples by train to Trecase, up the historic Matrone route to the crater, then bus down to Ercolano and back to Naples by evening.

09:30

Leave Naples

🚆 Circumvesuviana / EAV · ~40 min · €3.30

Board at Napoli Porta Nolana or Piazza Garibaldi. Take the Sorrento line toward Torre Annunziata and change — or take a direct service — to Trecase.

Buy your ticket before boarding. Validate it at the yellow machine on the platform.
10:15

Arrive Trecase — stock up

☕ On foot in the village · 15 min

Your last chance for coffee, breakfast, water, and a packed lunch. Buy at least 2.5–3 L of water per person here — there is nothing reliable on the trail.

Try Spagnuolo Caffè (Via Regina Margherita) or Bar Tabacchi Vitiello (Piazza S. Gennaro) for coffee and sandwiches.

Use the toilet here. The Park states there are currently no public toilets at Quota 1,000.
10:30

Trecase → Matrone trailhead

🚶 5.3 km road walk · ~1 h 15 min — or taxi (~€10)

A flat to gently rising road walk through the lower volcanic slopes. Perfectly manageable but exposed, so the early start helps. A taxi from the station saves an hour and arrives you fresher for the real climb.

11:45

Sentiero 6 — La Strada Matrone

🥾 ~8 km · ~900 m gain · ~3 hrs

The historic approach from the Trecase side — far quieter than the tourist route from Ercolano. The trail climbs through lava fields and sparse Mediterranean scrub, with increasingly dramatic views over the Bay of Naples as you gain height.

The route is currently listed as open by the Park. Check trail status the morning of your hike — temporary closures from weather or volcanic activity do occur.

~14:45

🌋 The crater — 1,175 m

🎟️ Pre-booked ticket required · €11.68 · open until 17:00

The Gran Cono: a 2 km circumference of volcanic sulphur vents, sheer inner walls dropping 300 m, and on a clear September day, views stretching from the Apennines to the islands of Capri and Ischia.

Book your slot at VivaTicket well in advance. No walk-up tickets. Arriving around 14:30–15:00 gives you a full hour at the top without rushing.

⚠️ The Park still shows €10 on one page — the current booking price via VivaTicket is €11.68. Budget the higher figure.
~16:00

Descend to Quota 1,000

🥾 ~1 km · 15–20 min

A short descent on Sentiero 5 to the Piazzale at Quota 1,000. With buses running every 10–20 minutes in high season, you won't wait long.

~16:20

Bus to Ercolano

🚌 EAV 808 · €3.10 · every 10–20 min

The bus descends from Quota 1,000 to Ercolano Osservatorio in about 20–25 minutes. From there it's a short walk to Ercolano Scavi station.

~17:30

Back in Naples

🚆 Circumvesuviana · ~35 min · €2.60

Train from Ercolano Scavi back to Napoli Piazza Garibaldi. Back in the city well before 18:00 — more than enough time for a well-earned aperitivo and a long dinner.
