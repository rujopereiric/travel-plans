# Netherlands Trip Planner

A day-by-day planner for the Netherlands. It works like the [Portugal planner](../portugal/README.md):
- opening hours and closing days;
- daylight;
- driving times;
- NS intercity trains between the cities (often faster than the car) and the Wadden island and Kinderdijk ferries where they beat the road.

It is one page with no backend and no build step, and works offline once loaded.

- `index.html`: the page and its styles
- `app.js`: all the logic. Everything specific to the Netherlands (currency, time zone, emergency numbers, help text, the OpenStreetMap regions) is in the `COUNTRY` block near the top and in `OSM_AREAS`. The rest of the file is the same in the Jordan, Uzbekistan, Ireland, Netherlands and Denmark planners, so copy fixes across.
- `data.json`: the places, regions, travel legs, links and checklist. Generated by `tools/assemble.py`, and also fine to edit by hand.
- `sw.js`: the service worker that handles offline use
- `parts/`: the regional research files that `data.json` is built from
- `tools/`: `assemble.py` builds `data.json`. `osm-update.mjs` prepares the "All of Netherlands" OpenStreetMap files.

## What's in it

There are 132 places in 8 regions, with 30 good overnight bases: Amsterdam, North Holland: Haarlem, Zaanse Schans & Texel, South Holland: Leiden, The Hague, Delft & Rotterdam, Utrecht, Zeeland, Veluwe, Arnhem & Overijssel, Friesland, Groningen & the Wadden, Brabant & Limburg.

Each place has a summary, practical facts, opening hours and closing days where they matter, and a Wikipedia photo.

- **Money:** prices are in euros.
- **Time:** Dutch time.
- **Defaults:** Up to 3.5 h of driving a day, 7 h of sightseeing, earliest start 08:30, car cost €0.25/km for fuel and parking. All can be changed in ⚙ Settings.

## Updating the places

The place data was written from general knowledge in October 2026, with the Wikipedia titles checked. Treat prices, opening hours and travel times as approximate, and check the official sites before you go.

To fix or add places:
1. Edit the file for that area in `parts/` (`west` and `east`). `parts/SPEC.md` describes the format.
2. Run `python3 netherlands/tools/assemble.py`.
3. Run `python3 trips/tools/build.py` to update the copy inside the Trips app.

## All of Netherlands (OpenStreetMap)

**Places → All of Netherlands** downloads every named sight from OpenStreetMap, region by region, and any of them can be added to your places.

The GitHub Action `.github/workflows/osm-netherlands.yml` prepares the files in `osm/`. It runs daily for missing or old regions, and can also be started by hand. Until it has run, the app asks the public Overpass servers directly.
