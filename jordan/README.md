# Jordan Trip Planner

A day-by-day planner for Jordan. It works like the [Portugal planner](../portugal/README.md):
- opening hours and closing days;
- daylight;
- driving times;
- the Amman ↔ Aqaba flight where they beat the road.

It is one page with no backend and no build step, and works offline once loaded.

- `index.html`: the page and its styles
- `app.js`: all the logic. Everything specific to Jordan (currency, time zone, emergency numbers, help text, the OpenStreetMap regions) is in the `COUNTRY` block near the top and in `OSM_AREAS`. The rest of the file is the same in the Jordan, Uzbekistan, Ireland, Netherlands and Denmark planners, so copy fixes across.
- `data.json`: the places, regions, travel legs, links and checklist. Generated by `tools/assemble.py`, and also fine to edit by hand.
- `sw.js`: the service worker that handles offline use
- `parts/`: the regional research files that `data.json` is built from
- `tools/`: `assemble.py` builds `data.json`. `osm-update.mjs` prepares the "All of Jordan" OpenStreetMap files.

## What's in it

There are 101 places in 8 regions, with 16 good overnight bases: Amman, Jerash, Ajloun & the north, Desert castles & Azraq, Dead Sea, Madaba & Mount Nebo, King's Highway: Kerak, Dana & Shobak, Petra & Wadi Musa, Wadi Rum, Aqaba.

Each place has a summary, practical facts, opening hours and closing days where they matter, and a Wikipedia photo.

- **Money:** prices are in dinars (JOD), with € alongside at a live rate (the currency converter is the € chip at the top).
- **Time:** Jordan time (UTC+3 all year).
- **Defaults:** Up to 5 h of driving a day, 7.5 h of sightseeing, earliest start 07:00, car cost 0.12 JOD/km for fuel. All can be changed in ⚙ Settings.

## Updating the places

The place data was written from general knowledge in October 2026, with the Wikipedia titles checked. Treat prices, opening hours and travel times as approximate, and check the official sites before you go.

To fix or add places:
1. Edit the file for that area in `parts/` (`north` and `south`). `parts/SPEC.md` describes the format.
2. Run `python3 jordan/tools/assemble.py`.
3. Run `python3 trips/tools/build.py` to update the copy inside the Trips app.

## All of Jordan (OpenStreetMap)

**Places → All of Jordan** downloads every named sight from OpenStreetMap, region by region, and any of them can be added to your places.

The GitHub Action `.github/workflows/osm-jordan.yml` prepares the files in `osm/`. It runs daily for missing or old regions, and can also be started by hand. Until it has run, the app asks the public Overpass servers directly.
