# Uzbekistan Trip Planner

A day-by-day planner for Uzbekistan. It works like the [Portugal planner](../portugal/README.md):
- opening hours and closing days;
- daylight;
- driving times;
- Afrosiyob and other trains, and domestic flights to Urgench, Nukus, Termez and Bukhara where they beat the road.

It is one page with no backend and no build step, and works offline once loaded.

- `index.html`: the page and its styles
- `app.js`: all the logic. Everything specific to Uzbekistan (currency, time zone, emergency numbers, help text, the OpenStreetMap regions) is in the `COUNTRY` block near the top and in `OSM_AREAS`. The rest of the file is the same in the Jordan, Uzbekistan, Ireland, Netherlands and Denmark planners, so copy fixes across.
- `data.json`: the places, regions, travel legs, links and checklist. Generated by `tools/assemble.py`, and also fine to edit by hand.
- `sw.js`: the service worker that handles offline use
- `parts/`: the regional research files that `data.json` is built from
- `tools/`: `assemble.py` builds `data.json`. `osm-update.mjs` prepares the "All of Uzbekistan" OpenStreetMap files.

## What's in it

There are 116 places in 9 regions, with 29 good overnight bases: Tashkent, Chimgan & Charvak mountains, Fergana Valley, Samarkand, Shahrisabz, Termez & the south, Nurata, Aydarkul & the Kyzylkum, Bukhara, Khiva & Khorezm, Nukus & the Aral Sea.

Each place has a summary, practical facts, opening hours and closing days where they matter, and a Wikipedia photo.

- **Money:** prices are in som (UZS), with € alongside at a live rate (the currency converter is the € chip at the top).
- **Time:** Uzbekistan time (UTC+5 all year; the sun rises ~35 min later in Khiva than in Tashkent).
- **Defaults:** Up to 6 h on the road a day with a private driver or shared taxi (rental cars are rare), 7.5 h of sightseeing, earliest start 07:30, 2,500 UZS/km for the driver. All can be changed in ⚙ Settings.

## Updating the places

The place data was written from general knowledge in October 2026, with the Wikipedia titles checked. Treat prices, opening hours and travel times as approximate, and check the official sites before you go.

To fix or add places:
1. Edit the file for that area in `parts/` (`east` and `west`). `parts/SPEC.md` describes the format.
2. Run `python3 uzbekistan/tools/assemble.py`.
3. Run `python3 trips/tools/build.py` to update the copy inside the Trips app.

## All of Uzbekistan (OpenStreetMap)

**Places → All of Uzbekistan** downloads every named sight from OpenStreetMap, region by region, and any of them can be added to your places.

The GitHub Action `.github/workflows/osm-uzbekistan.yml` prepares the files in `osm/`. It runs daily for missing or old regions, and can also be started by hand. Until it has run, the app asks the public Overpass servers directly.
