# Denmark Trip Planner

A day-by-day planner for Denmark. It works like the [Portugal planner](../portugal/README.md):
- opening hours and closing days;
- daylight;
- driving times;
- DSB intercity trains, the Bornholm, Ærø and Fanø ferries, and the Bornholm and Aalborg flights where they beat the road.

It is one page with no backend and no build step, and works offline once loaded.

- `index.html`: the page and its styles
- `app.js`: all the logic. Everything specific to Denmark (currency, time zone, emergency numbers, help text, the OpenStreetMap regions) is in the `COUNTRY` block near the top and in `OSM_AREAS`. The rest of the file is the same in the Jordan, Uzbekistan, Ireland, Netherlands and Denmark planners, so copy fixes across.
- `data.json`: the places, regions, travel legs, links and checklist. Generated by `tools/assemble.py`, and also fine to edit by hand.
- `sw.js`: the service worker that handles offline use
- `parts/`: the regional research files that `data.json` is built from
- `tools/`: `assemble.py` builds `data.json`. `osm-update.mjs` prepares the "All of Denmark" OpenStreetMap files.

## What's in it

There are 140 places in 9 regions, with 33 good overnight bases: Copenhagen, North Zealand: Helsingør, Hillerød & the Louisiana coast, Roskilde, Møn & South Zealand, Funen & the southern islands, Bornholm, South Jutland & the Wadden Sea, Aarhus, the Lake District & Legoland, West Jutland coast, North Jutland: Aalborg & Skagen.

Each place has a summary, practical facts, opening hours and closing days where they matter, and a Wikipedia photo.

- **Money:** prices are in kroner (DKK), with € alongside at a live rate (the currency converter is the € chip at the top).
- **Time:** Danish time.
- **Defaults:** Up to 4 h of driving a day, 7 h of sightseeing, earliest start 08:30, car cost 1.5 DKK/km for fuel (bridge tolls extra). All can be changed in ⚙ Settings.

## Updating the places

The place data was written from general knowledge in October 2026, with the Wikipedia titles checked. Treat prices, opening hours and travel times as approximate, and check the official sites before you go.

To fix or add places:
1. Edit the file for that area in `parts/` (`west` and `east`). `parts/SPEC.md` describes the format.
2. Run `python3 denmark/tools/assemble.py`.
3. Run `python3 trips/tools/build.py` to update the copy inside the Trips app.

## All of Denmark (OpenStreetMap)

**Places → All of Denmark** downloads every named sight from OpenStreetMap, region by region, and any of them can be added to your places.

The GitHub Action `.github/workflows/osm-denmark.yml` prepares the files in `osm/`. It runs daily for missing or old regions, and can also be started by hand. Until it has run, the app asks the public Overpass servers directly.
