How it works
  1. Tap a trip. Each one starts as a sample trip: set your own dates on its Plan tab (Change dates).
  2. Mark the days you are busy, star the places you want to see, then tap Auto-plan to fill your free days.
  3. Every trip has a ? Help button at the top right with the full guide.
  4. Your plans live only on this phone. Use Safety → Export JSON in a trip to back it up or send it to a friend.
