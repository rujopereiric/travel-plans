# Portugal with Kids

A family version of the [Portugal Trip Planner](../portugal/README.md): things to do in Portugal with children aged 0–14.
It uses the same engine as the Portugal planner (opening hours, daylight, driving times, flights to the islands, auto-plan),
but with a different set of places:
- zoos, aquariums, science centres, theme and water parks, farms, adventure parks and little trains;
- the castles, beaches, caves, boat trips and easy walks from the Portugal planner that families enjoy.

It is one page with no backend and no build step, and works offline once loaded.

## What's different from the Portugal planner

- **Kids' ages:** enter them at the top of the Places tab, or in ⚙ Settings (e.g. `2, 6, 9`). Every place has an age range
  (`ages: [min, max]`; 14 means "and older"). Suggestions and auto-plan only use places that suit **every** child.
  A ★ must-see is always allowed.
- **Buggy:** each place says whether a buggy works (`stroller`: yes / partly / no). Tick **We have a buggy** to leave out
  places where you'd need a baby carrier.
- **A tip for parents** on every card (`kids`), plus facilities (baby-changing, café, picnic area) where known.
- **🌧 Rainy day** filter on the Places tab: indoor places (aquariums, science centres, museums).
- **Gentler days by default:** up to 2.5 h of driving and 5.5 h of visits a day, starting at 09:00, with 45 min spare.
- **A family checklist and booking list:** children's ID, consent letters, car seats, sun and sea safety.

Your plan is saved under its own keys (`ptkids-…`), so it never mixes with your Portugal plan. Place photos, route
shapes and the **All of Portugal** OpenStreetMap downloads are shared with the Portugal planner, so they're not stored twice.
Offline caches are prefixed `ptkids-` (not `portugal-kids-`: the Portugal planner clears every cache starting with `portugal-`).

## Files

- `index.html`, `app.js`, `sw.js`: copies of the Portugal planner's, with the kids changes. A fix to the Portugal engine
  has to be copied here by hand.
- `data.json`: generated by `tools/assemble.py`. Don't edit it by hand.
- `parts/`: the kids research, one file per area. `parts/SPEC.md` describes the format.
- `tools/assemble.py`: builds `data.json` from `../portugal/data.json` and `parts/`.
  - It keeps the Portugal planner's regions, airports, overnight bases and travel legs.
  - It adds the kid fields to the Portugal places listed in each part's `reuse`.
  - It adds the new kid places (ids starting `k_`) and their road legs.

## Updating the places

The place data was written from general knowledge, with no web lookup. Treat prices, ages, opening hours and seasons as
approximate, and check the official sites before you go. Water parks and some theme parks only open in summer.

1. Edit the file for that area in `parts/`.
2. Run `python3 portugal-kids/tools/assemble.py`.
3. Run `python3 trips/tools/build.py` to update the copy inside the Trips app.

Re-run step 2 after changing the Portugal planner's data too, so the kids planner picks it up.
