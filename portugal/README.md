# Portugal Trip Planner

A day-by-day planner for Portugal: mainland Portugal, Madeira and the Azores. It was built from the [Egypt planner](../egypt/README.md) and works the same way:
- opening hours and closing days;
- daylight;
- driving times;
- flights, trains and ferries where they beat the car.

It is one page with no backend and no build step, and works offline once loaded.

- `index.html`: the page and its styles
- `app.js`: all the logic
- `data.json`: the places, regions, travel legs, links and checklist. Generated by `tools/assemble.py` (see below), and also fine to edit by hand.
- `sw.js`: the service worker that handles offline use
- `parts/`: the regional research files that `data.json` is built from
- `tools/`: `assemble.py` builds `data.json`. `osm-update.mjs` prepares the "All of Portugal" OpenStreetMap files.

## What's in it

There are 253 places in 12 regions, with 46 good overnight bases:
- **Mainland:** Lisbon; Sintra & Cascais; Setúbal & Arrábida; Óbidos, Nazaré & Tomar; Coimbra, Aveiro & Serra da Estrela; Porto; Minho & Gerês; Douro Valley & Trás-os-Montes; Alentejo; Algarve.
- **Islands:** Madeira and the Azores.

Each place has a summary, practical facts, opening hours and closing days where they matter (many museums close on Mondays), and a Wikipedia photo.

**Getting around:** the car is the default. The planner also uses these when they're faster than driving:
- flights from Lisbon and Porto to Madeira and the Azores;
- Azores inter-island flights and the Faial–Pico ferry;
- Alfa Pendular and Intercidades trains: Lisbon ↔ Coimbra ↔ Porto, and Lisbon ↔ Faro.

Their times are door to door.

## Differences from the Egypt planner

- **Currency:** everything is in euros, so there is no currency converter and no rate chip.
- **Time:** mainland Portugal time, including the summer clock change. Madeira uses the same clock. Azores days are shown in mainland time too; the Azores are 1 hour behind.
- **Defaults:** tuned for touring by car. Up to 4 h of driving a day, 6.5 h of sightseeing, earliest start 08:00, car cost €0.20/km for fuel and tolls.
- **Auto-plan speed:** with dozens of possible beds across the country, auto-plan tries only the 6 most promising beds per night. It also skips places that can't be worth a stop. On a desktop it takes a few seconds.

## Updating the places

The place data was written from general knowledge, with no web lookup. Treat prices, opening hours and drive times as approximate, and check the official sites before you go.

To fix or add places:
1. Edit the file for that area in `parts/` (`lisbon_sintra_setubal`, `oeste_centro`, `north`, `south` or `islands`). `parts/SPEC.md` describes the format.
2. Run `python3 portugal/tools/assemble.py`.
3. Run `python3 trips/tools/build.py` to update the copy inside the Trips app.

## Via ferratas

The via ferratas in My places come from OpenStreetMap. `tools/via_ferrata.py` reads the downloaded `osm/` files and merges the segments of each route. It names unnamed routes after the nearest landmark and writes `parts/via_ferrata.json`.

To refresh them after a new download, run:
```
python3 portugal/tools/via_ferrata.py && python3 portugal/tools/assemble.py
```

Routes that aren't mapped in OpenStreetMap won't appear. Add them to OpenStreetMap, or to a part file by hand.

## All of Portugal (OpenStreetMap)

**Places → All of Portugal** downloads every named castle, monastery, museum, beach, viewpoint, waterfall and so on, region by region. It also includes via ferratas and climbing crags. Any of them can be added to your places.

The GitHub Action `.github/workflows/osm-portugal.yml` prepares the files in `osm/`. It runs daily for missing or old regions, and can also be started by hand. Until it has run, the app asks the public Overpass servers directly.
